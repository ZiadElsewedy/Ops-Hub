1. Introduction

OpsHub (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a private, role-based operations management tool used by an organisation to run its branches and shifts — scheduling, task assignment, attendance, internal requests, and staff messaging.

Accounts are created for, and approved by, the organisation. The app is not a consumer social product and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.

By using OpsHub you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and contact your administrator.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what the app needs in order to work. That falls into the following categories.

Account information
Your name, email address, and the role and branch assigned to you by your administrator.
Profile details
Optional phone number, address, and emergency contact, where you or your administrator provide them.
Work activity
Tasks assigned to and completed by you, shift schedules, swap and leave requests, and the timestamps of those actions.
Attendance & location
Clock-in and clock-out times. If your organisation has enabled branch geofencing, an approximate location reading is taken at the moment you clock in or out to confirm you are at your branch. We do not track location in the background or when the app is closed.
Photos & attachments
Images or files you upload as proof of completed work, or attach to a request or conversation.
Messages
Content of staff conversations and case discussions you take part in inside the app.
Device & technical data
A push notification token for your device, app version, platform, and diagnostic or crash information.

We do not collect payment card details, government identification documents, contacts, biometrics, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to operate the service for your organisation:

  • Authentication and access — signing you in and showing you only what your role permits.
  • Scheduling and tasks — publishing your shifts, assigning work, and recording completion.
  • Attendance records — producing accurate clock-in/out and attendance reports for your branch.
  • Location verification — confirming a clock-in happened at the correct branch, when geofencing is enabled.
  • Notifications — alerting you to assignments, approvals, messages, and reminders.
  • Communication — delivering the internal messages and case discussions you participate in.
  • Reliability and security — diagnosing crashes, preventing abuse, and keeping accounts secure.

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond running the app for your organisation, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers listed in section 5, or where the law requires it.

4. Data Storage & Security

Where data lives

Data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure operated through Firebase. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS, and data at rest is encrypted by the platform.

Who can see it

Access is enforced server-side by security rules, not just by what the app chooses to display. Employees see their own records and their own branch's schedule; managers see their branch; administrators see the organisation. Conversations marked confidential are limited to their participants.

On your device

The app caches some data locally so it remains usable offline. That local cache is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Retention

Operational records — attendance, tasks, schedules, and requests — are retained for as long as your organisation needs them for business and record-keeping purposes. When your account is deleted, your profile and credentials are removed; historical operational records may be retained in a form tied to your organisation's records.

No system is perfectly secure. We apply industry-standard safeguards, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential and report any suspected compromise immediately.

5. Third-Party Services

OpsHub is built on Google Firebase. These services process data on our behalf, under Google's terms and privacy commitments:

Firebase Authentication
Manages sign-in and account credentials.
Cloud Firestore
Stores tasks, schedules, attendance, requests, and messages.
Firebase Storage
Stores photos and file attachments you upload.
Cloud Functions
Runs server-side logic such as approvals, reminders, and notification delivery.
Firebase Cloud Messaging
Delivers push notifications, via Apple Push Notification service on iOS.
Firebase Hosting
Serves this Privacy Policy page.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics brokers, or data resellers of any kind.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct — update your profile details in the app, or ask an administrator to correct them.
  • Delete — delete your account from the app's settings, or request deletion by contacting us.
  • Withdraw permissions — revoke location, camera, photo, or notification access at any time in your device settings. Some features, such as geofenced clock-in, will stop working without them.
  • Object or restrict — ask us to limit how your information is processed, where the law provides that right.
  • Complain — raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

Because OpsHub is an internal tool, some records are held by your organisation as the data controller. Requests about employment records may be directed to your administrator. We respond to requests sent to the address below within 30 days.
